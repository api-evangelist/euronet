# Euronet Worldwide

> Euronet Worldwide (NASDAQ: EEFT, Leawood, Kansas) is a global payments and financial technology company spanning Payments Infrastructure (EFT/ATM networks, processing, acquiring, REN software), epay (prepaid/digital media), and Money Transfer (Ria, Xe, Dandelion). Its most API-native surface is the Xe brand, which publishes real Swagger 2.0 developer APIs, plus Dandelion, an ISO 20022-compliant cross-border payout network. This file is generated by API Evangelist from the apis.io catalog.

## APIs

- [Xe Currency Data API](https://xecdapi.xe.com/docs/v1/): Real-time and historical FX rates for 170+ currencies. REST/JSON, HTTP Basic auth, self-serve free trial. Base: https://xecdapi.xe.com/v1
- [Xe Payments API](https://www.xe.com/platform/payments-api/): Cross-border payment execution (quotes, recipients, transactions) to 200+ countries. Partner-onboarded, sandbox available.
- [Xe Mass Payments API](https://www.xe.com/platform/payments-api/): Batch/bulk cross-border payouts (accounts, quotes, invoices, payments).
- [Xe Currency Data Tradable Rates API](https://app.swaggerhub.com/apis/XE.com/xecdapi-tradable/1.0.0): Tradable (dealable) FX rates companion.
- [XETA API](https://app.swaggerhub.com/apis/XE.com/xeta/v1.0.0): Xe trip/expense app surface (budget, expense, trip, user).
- [Dandelion Cross-Border Payments API](https://public.dandelionpayments.com/): Real-time ISO 20022 cross-border payouts (bank, wallet, cash pickup) to 190+ countries. Console login-gated.
